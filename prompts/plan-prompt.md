tools, then write a LIVING plan, not the whole story. Include:

- Premise: a short paragraph on what the story is and its hook.
- Overarching direction: a few loose lines on where it is generally headed, only as far as the
  idea actually supports. Do not invent a whole plot.
- Near-term detail: a beat outline ONLY for the part that is actually known (e.g. the first arc,
  up to the next chapter or two): what happens, who is present, what changes.

Outline only, no prose. It is fine, and expected, to leave the later story vague; the plan grows
as you write. Do not pad it into a full chapter-by-chapter breakdown of the whole book.
