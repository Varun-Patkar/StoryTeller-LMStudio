# Canon timeline

This is the true storyline of the source, the world of Kingdom Come as it plays out across
both games. Treat everything here as fact unless the plan says otherwise. The plan is where
this story changes things. This file does not.

Dates are given the way the games give them. The year is 1403 in the Kingdom of Bohemia, part
of the Holy Roman Empire.

## The wider war

The old Emperor Charles IV is dead. He left two sons. Wenceslaus IV, the elder, holds the
Bohemian crown, but he is a weak and idle king and the lords do not respect him. His younger
half-brother, Sigismund of Luxembourg, King of Hungary, takes him prisoner and seizes power in
his name. To hold the land, Sigismund brings in Cuman mercenaries, cruel horsemen from the
east, and lets them burn and loot their way across Bohemia. A league of loyal lords wants
Wenceslaus back on the throne. This is the war that swallows Henry's life.

## Kingdom Come: Deliverance (the first game)

### The fall of Skalitz

Henry is the son of Martin, the best blacksmith in Silver Skalitz, a small mining town rich in
silver and held by Sir Radzig Kobyla. Henry is a bit of a layabout at the start, fond of
drink and brawling. His father is forging a fine sword for Sir Radzig.

Sigismund's army falls on Skalitz without warning. It is a force of Cuman mercenaries led by Sir
Markvart von Aulitz, Sigismund's trusted general. The town is put to the sword and the torch.
Markvart himself cuts down Henry's father Martin and, moments later, his mother, both in front of
Henry, who is frozen in terror. Henry's sweetheart Bianca is killed in the raid as well, along
with much of the town. Henry runs. He escapes on horseback up to Talmberg Castle, held by Sir
Divish and Lady Stephanie, and collapses at the gate.

### Rattay and the road back

Once Henry is on his feet, he goes back down to ruined Skalitz to bury his parents and take his
father's sword. He is caught and beaten by the bandits Runt and Zbyshek and the sword is stolen,
and he is nearly killed. He is saved when Theresa, a girl who also survived the raid, and Sir
Robard of Talmberg drive the bandits off and tend him. Henry makes his way to Rattay, the town of
Sir Hanush of Leipa, where Sir Radzig has gathered what forces remain.

Henry takes service under Captain Bernard, the garrison's hard but fair master-at-arms, who
teaches him to fight properly. Sir Radzig takes an interest in the boy. Henry swears himself to
Radzig's service and sets out to hunt the men who destroyed his home.

### Hunting the raiders

Henry chases the trail of the bandits and the Cumans across the region. Key threads:

- The stud farm at Neuhof is raided and its horses stolen. Henry helps set it right.
- The village of Merhojed is struck by pestilence and Henry must find the cause and a cure.
- Henry befriends the young lord Hans Capon, a proud and reckless noble. On a hunt the two are
  ambushed and taken by Cumans, and must escape together. The friendship holds from then on.
- At Uzhitz, Henry deals with the drunken priest Father Godwin, a bandit witch-hunt, and other
  small troubles of village life.
- Henry goes undercover as a novice monk in Sasau Monastery to hunt a hidden enemy, living the
  monk's day and night while he searches.

Through all this Henry closes on Runt, corners him, and learns the raid was no simple bandit
work. It was ordered. Behind it stands a Hungarian nobleman and spy, Istvan Toth, called the
Chief, a loyal servant of Sigismund. Toth runs a hidden bandit camp at Vranik, deep in the
woods, raising a small army.

### Vranik, Talmberg, and the truth

Henry scouts Vranik and brings word back. The lords gather to strike the camp. But Toth is a
step ahead. He takes his men to Talmberg, storms the castle, and takes Lady Stephanie and Sir
Radzig himself as hostages.

The lords lay siege to Talmberg. With the help of the siege engineer Konrad Kyeser, a trebuchet
is built and the walls are broken. In the fighting Toth strikes a bargain. Sir Hanush gives his
word that Toth's men may ride out free if the hostages are set down safely in the countryside.
A lord's word must be kept, so Toth is let go, and Sir Radzig is found safe in a meadow outside
Skalitz. Henry and Hans ride off after Toth, who is now fair game.

Before the end, Henry learns the truth of his own blood. Sir Radzig is his true father. Henry
was raised as a blacksmith's son but he is a nobleman's bastard.

### The lords' errand

The game closes at Rattay. Sir Radzig, Sir Hanush, Sir Divish, Jobst of Moravia, and John of
Liechtenstein meet to plan the war for Wenceslaus. They need Otto von Bergow, Lord of Trosky
and a high man in Sigismund's council, to come over to their side. They send Henry and Hans
Capon east to Trosky to deliver a letter and take his measure. The two ride out. That errand is
where the second game begins.

## Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (the second game)

### The ambush in the Bohemian Paradise

Henry and Hans ride into the rocky country called the Bohemian Paradise with a small escort.
They are ambushed. The escort is massacred, the two are wounded and stripped of everything,
and the lords' letter is lost. The men who did it turn out to be a band of outlaws led by the
robber-knight Jan Žižka, who fights in secret for the imprisoned Wenceslaus. Žižka's men are no
common bandits. When one of them tries to force himself on the woman Katherine, Žižka kills his
own man for it.

Henry and Hans are found and healed by the herbalist Bozena. Separated and then reunited, they
fall into the hands of Otto von Bergow at Trosky Castle, who forces them to do his bidding.

### Nebakov and the duel

Von Bergow sends Henry and Hans to Nebakov to deal with outlaws. There the truth comes clear.
The outlaws are the armed hand of the loyalists fighting for Wenceslaus. Henry and Hans throw
in with Žižka's people. Their old enemy Istvan Toth surfaces here too, still serving Sigismund.
After the battle at Nebakov Mill, Henry and Žižka come to blows in a duel, and Henry wounds
Žižka's left eye. The eye is later saved by Father Godwin.

Henry, Godwin, and Žižka are thrown into the dungeons of Trosky, while Hans is taken prisoner by
von Bergow. They escape. In Trosky's tower, Henry and Istvan Toth have their final reckoning,
and Henry throws Toth from a high window to his death.

### Suchdol and Kuttenberg

Henry travels with Žižka and Katherine to Suchdol, where he meets Peter of Písek and speaks
again with Jobst of Moravia. From there he goes to the great silver city of Kuttenberg.

In Kuttenberg Henry follows a tangle of threads: a spy for Sigismund's man Lord John of
Liechtenstein, a fraud in the silver mines and the mint, and the powerful merchant family of
the Ruthards, Kunzlin and his daughter Rosa. He uncovers a hidden man named Samuel, who proves
to be Martin's own illegitimate son, and so a half-brother to Henry. Henry works to rescue Hans
Capon, who is being held at Maleszov, and frees him.

### The turning

Sigismund moves to seize the property of Kuttenberg's Jews. Henry races to warn his friends and,
with Samuel, Yehuda, and others, works to save the Jewish families and the woman Sara. He and
Hans gather their strength at Devil's Den, and Henry proves himself in a great tournament in
Kuttenberg.

### The Battle of Suchdol

The war comes to a head at Suchdol, where the loyalist cause makes its stand against Sigismund's
forces and Otto von Bergow. The fighting decides the fate of the region. Henry's road through
the two games ends here, and the way is opened toward the Hussite age to come, in which Žižka
will make his name as one of the great commanders of the world. That is beyond the story.

## The people the story turns on

- **Henry of Skalitz.** The hero of both games. Raised a blacksmith's son, revealed to be the
  bastard of Sir Radzig Kobyla. Squire and best friend to Hans Capon.
- **Sir Radzig Kobyla.** Lord of Skalitz, loyalist leader, Henry's true father.
- **Hans Capon.** Young loyalist lord, proud and rash, Henry's closest friend.
- **Captain Bernard.** Rattay's master-at-arms who trains Henry.
- **Sir Hanush of Leipa.** Lord of Rattay, blunt and shrewd loyalist.
- **Sir Divish and Lady Stephanie of Talmberg.** Lord and lady of Talmberg.
- **Theresa.** Survivor of Skalitz, Henry's sweetheart.
- **Father Godwin.** Worldly, drinking priest and a true friend.
- **Istvan Toth.** Hungarian spy and Sigismund's man, the hand behind the Skalitz raid, chief
  villain of the first game. His lover and protégé is Erik.
- **Sir Markvart von Aulitz.** Sigismund's trusted general who leads the sack of Skalitz and
  personally kills Henry's parents. Henry's chief target for revenge across both games.
- **Bianca.** Henry's sweetheart in Skalitz, killed in the raid.
- **Runt.** The bandit captain who beats Henry at the Skalitz ruins and steals his father's sword.
- **Jan Žižka.** Robber-knight and secret loyalist, later the famed Hussite general.
- **Otto von Bergow.** Lord of Trosky, high man to Sigismund, chief villain of the second game.
- **Katherine, Kubyenka, Dry Devil, Janosh.** Žižka's trusted companions.
- **Rosa Ruthard and Kunzlin Ruthard.** Kuttenberg merchant family.
- **Samuel.** Martin's illegitimate son, a hidden half-brother to Henry.
- **Jobst of Moravia and John of Liechtenstein.** Great lords on opposite sides of the war.
