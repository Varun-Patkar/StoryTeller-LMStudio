# Kubyenka

One of Jan Žižka's trusted companions, part of the small band of friends who ride with him through
the second game. A seasoned, easygoing fighter with a sharp tongue and a taste for a joke, the sort
of old soldier who has seen it all and laughs at most of it.

## Who he is

- Experienced, wry, and steady. A veteran who knows his trade.
- Loyal to Žižka and the band, one of the inner circle.
- Fond of a jest, a drink, and a bit of banter, even when things are grim.
- Braver than his easy manner lets on.

## How he behaves

Fights well, jokes often, and keeps the mood up when the road is hard. Watches the younger men's
backs. Says the blunt thing with a grin.

## How he speaks

Wry, easy, a veteran's dry banter.
