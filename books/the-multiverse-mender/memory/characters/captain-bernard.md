# Captain Bernard

The master-at-arms of the Rattay garrison. A hard, blunt, experienced soldier who teaches Henry
how to truly fight. He does not coddle and he does not flatter. Everything Henry knows about the
sword, the shield, and staying alive, he owes to Bernard's drilling.

For Hugo, Bernard is a name to know well: he is the man who comes to save Henry at the right
moment, the one Hugo must hold the line until he arrives.

## Who he is

- Gruff, plain-spoken, and impatient with excuses. Suffers no fools on his training ground.
- A true professional soldier. Knows war for the ugly trade it is and teaches it straight.
- Fair underneath the barking. Respects sweat, courage, and a man who keeps getting back up.
- Loyal to the garrison and the cause.

## How he behaves

Drills recruits until they drop, then drills them again. Corrects with a sharp word and a sharper
blow. Grudgingly proud when a man improves. Steady and deadly in a real fight.

## How he speaks

Short, blunt, soldier's talk. No poetry, all point.
