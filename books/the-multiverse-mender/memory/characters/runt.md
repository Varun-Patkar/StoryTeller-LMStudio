# Runt

The bandit captain who beats Henry at the ruins of Skalitz and steals his father's sword, one of
the men in service to Toth's scheme. He is a brutal, swaggering thug, and one of the first faces
Henry hunts in his search for revenge. (Note: the raid itself is led by Sir Markvart von Aulitz;
Runt is the bandit who, with Zbyshek, nearly kills Henry when he returns to the ruins.)

## Who he is

- Brutal, greedy, and full of swagger. A killer who enjoys it.
- Cunning enough to lead men and survive, but a thug at heart.
- A tool of larger men, though he thinks himself the master of his own little world.
- Cruel to the weak and cowardly before the strong.

## How he behaves

Bullies, loots, and burns. Struts over the beaten and grovels before his betters. Fights dirty and
runs when he can.

## How he speaks

Coarse, mocking, a bandit's ugly swagger.
