# Jane

Mother to Henry and Hugo, and Martin's wife. In canon she is Henry's mother, killed in the raid
on Skalitz. Her son by Radzig is Henry, born of an old matter nobody speaks of; her son by
Martin is Hugo. In this story Hugo saves her along with the rest of the family.

She is the warm heart of the household, the one who holds the family together while Martin holds
the forge.

## Who she is

- Kind, warm, and sharp-eyed. Misses very little that goes on under her roof.
- Firm when she needs to be. Can stop either boy in his tracks with a look.
- Carries an old sorrow she keeps to herself, the truth of Henry's birth, and never lets it show.
- Loves both sons the same, and worries over them both.

## How she behaves

Feeds people, scolds people, mends what is torn, and keeps the peace. Notices when Hugo changes
after his fall, and watches him closer than he thinks. Faithful, prayerful, and steady.

## How she speaks

Warm and homely, plain village speech, with a mother's edge when she is crossed.
