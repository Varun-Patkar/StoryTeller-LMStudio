# Sir Hanush of Leipa

Lord of Rattay, uncle to Hans Capon, and one of the loyalist leaders. A blunt, shrewd, hard-drinking
noble with a sharp tongue and a sharper head for politics. He and Sir Radzig do not always agree,
but they hold the cause together between them. It is Hanush who gives his word to let Toth go for
the hostages, because a lord's word must be kept.

## Who he is

- Gruff, loud, and clever. Hides a keen political mind behind rough manners.
- Proud and stubborn, quick to argue, slow to back down.
- Loyal to the cause and to his blood, in his own gruff way.
- A man of his word, and he takes that seriously, even when it costs him.

## How he behaves

Argues with the other lords, drinks more than he should, and still runs the region well. Barks at
Henry but comes to respect him. Plays the long game of noble politics with skill.

## How he speaks

Loud, blunt, impatient, a great lord who talks like a soldier. Verified line from the game:

- "Your graces, I have to tell you in all seriousness, that this land of ours is in the shit.
  Deep, fucking shit."
