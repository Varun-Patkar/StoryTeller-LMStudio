# Rosa Ruthard

Daughter of the wealthy Kuttenberg merchant Kunzlin Ruthard. A clever, spirited young woman caught
in the web of her father's business and the city's politics. In canon she is one of Henry's
possible loves in the second game. In this story the fling belongs to Hugo instead.

Hugo and Rosa share a brief, honest thing, and both of them know from the start that it goes
nowhere. For her it is a matter of station: a merchant's daughter does not end up with a common
adventurer. For Hugo it is that he will not be in this world forever, though he can never tell her
why. It is warmth and want and a little escape for them both, freely given, with no lies about what
it is.

## Who she is

- Sharp, proud, and quick-witted. More than a match for most men in a conversation.
- Trapped, a little, by her father's world and the expectations of her station.
- Warm and passionate under the polished merchant-daughter surface.
- Clear-eyed. She knows what she wants and what she cannot have, and does not pretend otherwise.

## How she behaves

Runs rings around men who underestimate her. Takes what pleasure and freedom she can. Guards her
heart even while she gives her body. Says the hard true thing rather than the sweet false one.

## How she speaks

Clever, teasing, frank, with a merchant's edge and a woman's honesty.
