# Dry Devil

One of Jan Žižka's band of trusted companions in the second game. A hard, colourful fighter with a
fearsome name and a reputation to match. One of the rough brotherhood around Žižka who do the ugly
work of the loyalist cause in the woods and the shadows.

## Who he is

- Tough, blunt, and a little wild. Earned his name and enjoys it.
- Loyal to Žižka and the band above all.
- A brawler and a survivor, more comfortable in a fight than a conversation.
- Rough company, but a good man to have at your back.

## How he behaves

Fights hard, drinks hard, and speaks his mind. Stands by his own. Not one for subtlety.

## How he speaks

Gruff, coarse, plain, a hard man's short speech.
