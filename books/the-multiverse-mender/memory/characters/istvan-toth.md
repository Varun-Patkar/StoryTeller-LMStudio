# Istvan Toth

The chief villain of the first game, called the Chief. A Hungarian nobleman, spy, and mercenary,
and a loyal servant of King Sigismund. He is the hand behind the raid on Skalitz and the death of
Henry's parents. Cold, clever, ruthless, and utterly without mercy.

Born in Banat on the Hungarian border, orphaned young when Turks killed his parents, he turned his
whole life into a weapon. He saved Sigismund's life at Nicopolis and earned his title and place. In
his raiding days he orphaned a boy named Erik and raised him into his most trusted man and his
lover.

In canon Toth lives on into the second game and dies at Trosky when Henry throws him from a tower
window. (Note for this story: the plan brings his death forward.)

## Who he is

- Cold, calculating, and cruel. Sees people as tools or obstacles.
- Brave and capable, a real soldier and spy, not a mere thug.
- Utterly loyal to Sigismund and to his own ambition. Loyal, in his way, only to Erik.
- Enjoys power and the fear it buys him. Contemptuous of the weak.

## How he behaves

Plans in the shadows and strikes without warning. Uses others and discards them. Keeps his temper
and his cunning even cornered. Cruel by choice, not just by trade.

## How he speaks

Cold, mocking, contemptuous, and deliberately crude. Verified line from the game (his creed, and
his dying words):

- "The stronger dog fucks the bitches."
