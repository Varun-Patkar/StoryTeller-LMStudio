# Hans Capon

A young loyalist lord, nephew to Sir Hanush, and in time Henry's closest friend. He starts out
proud, spoiled, and reckless, a highborn boy who has never had to earn a thing. Through the two
games he grows up, and the bond between him and Henry becomes the beating heart of the whole
story. By the second game Henry is his squire, and the two are near inseparable.

## Who he is

- Proud, vain, and rash at the start. Quick to give orders and slow to think.
- Brave, though, and loyal once he lets you in. There is a good man under the fine clothes.
- Loves a hunt, a drink, a woman, and a fight, not always in that order.
- Grows into a real leader, humbled and steadied by everything he goes through with Henry.

## How he behaves

Swaggers, boasts, gets the pair of them into trouble, then fights his way out beside Henry. Trades
insults with Henry like a brother. Learns, slowly, that a title is not the same as worth.

## How he speaks

Highborn, flip, teasing, full of himself, but warmer as time goes on. Verified lines from the
game:

- "I am Sir Hans Capon of Pirkstein!" (his furious cry when no one will believe his rank)
- "Audentes fortuna iuvat! Fortune favours the brave!" (the motto he and Henry live by)
