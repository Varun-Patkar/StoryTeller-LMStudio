# Theresa

A young woman of Skalitz who survives the raid alongside Henry, and in time his sweetheart. Brave,
kind, and steadier than she looks. In the raid she and Henry hide and help each other live, and
their bond grows from there. In this story she is one of the hands that helps save Henry when he
goes back for the survivors, holding on beside Hugo until Captain Bernard comes.

She remains Henry's love. Her road and Hugo's do not cross that way.

## Who she is

- Gentle but tough. Grief and fear do not break her; they steady her.
- Practical and warm. Good with her hands, good with people, no stranger to hard work.
- Devoted to Henry, and quietly courageous when it counts.
- Faithful and hopeful even after losing nearly everything.

## How she behaves

Keeps her feet under her when the world falls apart. Tends the hurt, calms the frightened, and
stands her ground when she has to. Shy about her feelings for Henry, but sure of them.

## How she speaks

Soft, honest, plain village speech, with more steel in it than strangers expect.
