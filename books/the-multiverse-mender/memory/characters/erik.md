# Erik

Istvan Toth's most trusted man, his protégé and his lover. Toth orphaned Erik in a raid years ago
and then raised him, and Erik has been his shadow ever since. Younger, sharp, and dangerous, a
capable fighter and a loyal knife in Toth's hand.

In this story Erik is present at the ambush of Henry and Hans on the road to Trosky, but he
recognises Hans and his band and slips away rather than fight them then. His end comes later, in the
second game.

## Who he is

- Loyal to Toth above all else, bound to him by upbringing and by love.
- Cold and capable, but with more feeling than his master, especially where Toth is concerned.
- Proud, wary, and quick. Not a brute; a thinking, dangerous man.
- Carries the mark of the man who made him, for good and ill.

## How he behaves

Watches, waits, and acts on Toth's word. Fiercely protective of him. Knows when to fight and when
to vanish. Cooler-headed than most of the men around him.

## How he speaks

Clipped, wary, controlled. Warmer only when Toth is the subject.
