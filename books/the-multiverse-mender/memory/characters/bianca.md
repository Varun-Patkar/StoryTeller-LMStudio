# Bianca

Henry's sweetheart in Silver Skalitz before the raid. A young woman of the town, warm and full of
life, and the first great loss of Henry's story. She is killed when Markvart's men sack Skalitz.

In this story she still dies in the raid. Hugo can save his own family, but he cannot save
everyone, and Bianca is one of the ones lost. Her death becomes a large part of what drives
Henry's grief and his hunger for revenge, in place of the parents he does not lose here.

## Who she is

- Young, warm, and fond of Henry. An ordinary girl of the town with an ordinary, hopeful life
  ahead of her, cut short.
- A symbol, after her death, of everything the war takes from the small people who never asked
  for it.

## How she behaves

Sweet on Henry, teasing and affectionate in the short time we see her, before the raid ends it.

## How she speaks

Sweet and teasing with Henry in the little we see of her before the raid.
