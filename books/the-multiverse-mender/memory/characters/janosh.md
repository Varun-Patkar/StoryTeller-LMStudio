# Janosh

One of Jan Žižka's trusted companions in the second game, part of the tight band of friends who
ride and fight with him. A capable, loyal fighter, another of the rough brotherhood that holds the
loyalist cause together in the field.

## Who he is

- Steady, loyal, and capable. A dependable man in a fight.
- One of Žižka's inner circle, bound to the band by hard shared roads.
- Plain and practical, with a soldier's patience.
- Not showy, but the sort who is always there when it counts.

## How he behaves

Does his part without fuss. Stands with the band through thick and thin. Keeps his head and his
word.

## How he speaks

Plain, practical, a working soldier's speech.
