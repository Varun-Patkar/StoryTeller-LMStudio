# Sir Divish of Talmberg

Lord of Talmberg Castle, husband to Lady Stephanie, and a loyalist ally. It is his gate Henry
reaches when Skalitz falls, and in this story his walls that shelter the family Hugo saves. Later
his castle is stormed and taken by Istvan Toth, and Divish suffers the shame and grief of losing
his home and seeing his wife taken hostage.

## Who he is

- Proud, formal, and conscious of his rank and his honour.
- Loyal to the cause, if sometimes prickly about how he is treated by the greater lords.
- Genuinely fond of his wife, and shaken hard when she is put in danger.
- Brave enough, though a lord of walls more than of the open field.

## How he behaves

Holds his court with proper dignity. Grants shelter and aid as a lord should. Bristles at slights.
Fights with real anger once his own home and wife are threatened.

## How he speaks

Formal, proud, a nobleman's careful speech.
