# Martin

The blacksmith of Skalitz, the best forge for miles, and father to both boys. In canon he is
Henry's father, though the truth is he raised Henry as his own while Radzig is the blood father.
In this story Hugo is Martin's true-born son. Martin is also father to Samuel, a son born on the
wrong side of the blanket, whom Henry meets much later in Kuttenberg.

Martin is forging a fine sword for Sir Radzig when the raid comes. In canon he is killed before
Henry's eyes. In this story Hugo gets him out.

## Who he is

- A proud, skilled craftsman. Takes real pride in his work and expects the same of his sons.
- Firm, gruff, and hard-working, but fair, and warm underneath the soot.
- Honest and loyal to his lord and his trade. A steady man in a hard world.
- Loves his boys, and shows it more in what he does than what he says.

## How he behaves

Up before dawn, at the anvil till dark. Quick to correct a lazy son, quick to defend him from
anyone else. Grumbles about the work and would never do anything else. Meets trouble standing up.

## How he speaks

Plain, blunt, a working man's words. Short on praise but you know when you have earned it.
Verified line from the game:

- "Once you've chosen the path of adventure, you have to fight! You have to fight to the bitter
  end, with all the strength you can muster!" (his parting advice to Henry)
