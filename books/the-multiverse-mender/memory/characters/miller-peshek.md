# Miller Peshek

The miller of Rattay, and one of Henry's more colourful helpers in the first game. Millers in this
world live half in honest trade and half in the shady one, dealing with thieves, fences, and stolen
goods, and Peshek is no exception. He teaches Henry the useful, dishonest arts: lockpicking,
sneaking, and moving things nobody should be moving. Friendly, sly, and good company.

## Who he is

- Sly, cheerful, and light-fingered. A rogue with a good heart.
- Well-connected in the region's underworld of millers and thieves.
- Loyal to friends and generous once he likes you.
- Practical about the law, which is to say he works around it.

## How he behaves

Runs the mill by day and other business by night. Takes Henry under his wing for the shadier
skills. Winks at the law and helps friends out of scrapes.

## How he speaks

Cheerful, sly, a working rogue's easy patter.
