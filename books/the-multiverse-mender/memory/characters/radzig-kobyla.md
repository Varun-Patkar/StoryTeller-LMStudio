# Sir Radzig Kobyla

Lord of Skalitz, a leader of the loyalist lords, and Henry's true father. A seasoned nobleman and
soldier who carries himself with quiet authority. He kept the truth of Henry's birth hidden for
years, and only near the end of the first game does Henry learn that the lord he serves is his own
blood.

## Who he is

- Grave, measured, and shrewd. A man used to command and to keeping secrets.
- Honourable in the hard, practical way of his class. Duty first, feeling second, though the
  feeling is real.
- Carries guilt over Henry, over the son he could not claim and the mother he could not keep.
- A capable war leader who holds the loyalist cause together.

## How he behaves

Speaks little and means all of it. Weighs people before he trusts them. Gives Henry chances and
watches to see what he makes of them. Keeps his own counsel even when it costs him.

## How he speaks

Formal, controlled, a lord's measured speech. Warmth only in rare, quiet moments. Verified line
from the game:

- "Life is one long series of problems to solve. The more you solve, the better a man you
  become."
