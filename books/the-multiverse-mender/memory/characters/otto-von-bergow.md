# Otto von Bergow

Lord of Trosky Castle, Royal Chamberlain and a high man in King Sigismund's council, and the chief
villain of the second game. A powerful, cold, calculating feudal lord who holds Henry and Hans in
his grip after their ambush and forces them to do his bidding. He is the loyalists' target in the
end: the man they hoped to win over, who instead stands with Sigismund at the last.

## Who he is

- Powerful, proud, and cold. A great lord used to bending others to his will.
- Political and self-serving. Backs whichever side serves his power and his lands.
- Ruthless with those beneath him, courteous with those above.
- Dangerous less with a blade than with a word and a writ.

## How he behaves

Rules Trosky as a small king. Uses Henry and Hans as pawns. Keeps his own counsel and his own
interests first. Meets the war as a matter of leverage, not honour.

## How he speaks

Lordly, cold, precise, a man who never raises his voice because he never has to.
