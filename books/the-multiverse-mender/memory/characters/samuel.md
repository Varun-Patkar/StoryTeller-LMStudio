# Samuel

A hidden man Henry uncovers in Kuttenberg in the second game, who turns out to be the illegitimate
son of Martin the blacksmith, and so a half-brother to Henry. A Jew of the city, tangled in the
threads of the silver, the spies, and Sigismund's cruelty toward the Jewish families. Finding him
is one of the quiet gut-punches of the second game: a piece of Henry's dead father, alive and a
stranger.

In this story the family tie runs deeper still, since Hugo is Martin's true-born son. Samuel shares
that blood.

## Who he is

- Careful, guarded, and clever. A man used to hiding who he is.
- Caught between worlds, the Christian city and the Jewish quarter, his father's people and his own.
- Brave under pressure, and decent when trust is earned.
- Carries the wary weariness of a man the world has never made room for.

## How he behaves

Keeps his cards close. Tests people before he opens up. Helps once he trusts, and risks much for
his own. Meets the revelation of kinship with caution, then something warmer.

## How he speaks

Measured, careful, a little weary, warming slowly.
