# Father Godwin

The priest of Uzhitz, and one of the most human men of the cloth you will ever meet. He drinks,
he gambles, he keeps company with women, and he is still, somehow, a genuinely good and wise
friend. He speaks plain sense under all the vice. In the second game it is Godwin's skill that
saves Jan Žižka's wounded eye.

## Who he is

- Worldly, funny, and fond of his cups. Sins openly and forgives easily.
- Wiser and kinder than his habits suggest. Sees people clearly and judges them gently.
- Brave and loyal when it matters, and handy in a scrape.
- Believes in God and in mercy, if not in his own good behaviour.

## How he behaves

Preaches with a hangover, drinks with sinners, and gives better counsel drunk than most men give
sober. Turns up when friends need him. Laughs at himself before anyone else can.

## How he speaks

Warm, wry, worldly, full of easy wisdom and easy jokes. The one verified moment from the game is
not a witty line but a solemn one: at the tower of Nebakov, with the enemy cannon aimed at them,
Godwin recites the Lord's Prayer in Latin, ending on "but deliver us from evil," just as the shot
lands.
