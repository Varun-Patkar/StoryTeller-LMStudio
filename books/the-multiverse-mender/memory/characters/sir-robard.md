# Sir Robard

The captain of the Talmberg garrison, a steady, professional soldier. He helps Henry early on,
including the business of getting Henry properly armed and horsed when he first needs to ride out.
A minor but solid figure of the loyalist side, the sort of reliable officer who keeps a castle
running.

## Who he is

- Disciplined, dutiful, and calm. A career man-at-arms.
- Fair and helpful to Henry within the bounds of his orders.
- Loyal to Sir Divish and to Talmberg.
- No-nonsense, but not unkind.

## How he behaves

Runs the garrison by the book. Gives Henry a hand where he can. Keeps his head in a crisis.

## How he speaks

Plain, steady, an officer's practical speech.
