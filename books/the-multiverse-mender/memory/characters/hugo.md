# Hugo

The main character. In his first life he was a young man who died with a controller in his hands
and woke in front of the god of these worlds. He kept his name. By the god's quiet design, he is
Hugo in every life he will ever live. Now he is Hugo of Skalitz, second son of Martin the
blacksmith and Jane, and by that the younger brother of Henry by one year.

His memory of who he was, and of the game he once played, does not wake until he is twelve, after
a fall that nearly kills him. From that day he carries two lives in one head: the boy raised at
the forge, and the man who knows exactly what is coming for this valley and everyone in it.

## Who he is

- Younger than Henry by a year. Eighteen when Skalitz burns.
- Raised at the forge, so real strength in his arms and a real trade in his hands. He knows iron.
- Sharp, watchful, and patient. He plans. He has to, because he has no proof of the future and no
  rank to make anyone listen. He is a smith's son, nothing more, in the eyes of the world.
- Loves his family fiercely and quietly. Saving them is the whole point. He will not lose them
  again the way the story says he should.
- Carries a modern man's mind under a plain medieval face. Dry, a little wry, slow to pray and
  quick to doubt, but he keeps that behind his teeth and speaks like the folk around him.

## The system

Hugo has a god-granted system that only he can see. He says the word "System" and a panel appears
showing his attributes and skills. It has no shop and gives nothing for free. He grows only by
doing the thing the honest way, over and over, and by finding and earning what he needs in the
world. Its ceiling is this world's ceiling: no magic, just the far edge of what a man can be.

## How he behaves

Keeps his head down and his eyes open. Trains in secret. Puts coin and steel aside before anyone
knows there is a war coming. Lets Henry walk his own road even when it hurts to watch, and steps
in only where he must. Guards his true nature like a wound. He will tell one person the truth, at
the very end, and then he is gone.

## How he speaks

Plain and local on the outside, dry and knowing on the inside.

- "I'm a smith's boy. I have to actually work for things unlike Henry here with a noble father."(jopking tone, after henry's lineage is revealed)
- "We're leaving before the smoke drives the Cumans here. Pack what you can carry and move."
- "I can't tell you how I know. You wouldn't believe me, and I wouldn't blame you. Just consider it god's grace to me."
- "Everyone here has a part to play. Mine is to make sure the ones I love get to live to play theirs."
