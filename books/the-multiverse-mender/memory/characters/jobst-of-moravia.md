# Jobst of Moravia

A great lord and a major player in the war for the throne, cousin to the royal brothers. He comes
to the loyalists to seek alliance against Sigismund and to restore King Wenceslaus. It is his
errand, along with the other lords, that sends Henry and Hans east to Trosky at the end of the
first game, the thread that carries into the second. Henry crosses paths with him again at Suchdol.

## Who he is

- A powerful, ambitious nobleman playing the great game of crowns.
- Shrewd, political, and used to being obeyed.
- An ally of convenience to the loyalist lords, with his own ends always in view.
- Dignified and careful, a man who weighs every word.

## How he behaves

Moves in the high politics of the realm. Makes and breaks alliances. Speaks carefully and expects
the same. Treats Henry as a useful instrument of greater men's plans.

## How he speaks

Lordly, measured, political, every word chosen.
