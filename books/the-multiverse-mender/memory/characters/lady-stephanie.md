# Lady Stephanie of Talmberg

Lady of Talmberg, wife to Sir Divish. A capable, gracious noblewoman who helps see Henry back onto
his feet after the raid. Later she is seized by Istvan Toth and held hostage in her own castle, one
of the hostages the lords must win back at the siege of Talmberg.

## Who she is

- Poised, kind, and clever. Runs her household with a firm, gentle hand.
- Brave under threat. Keeps her dignity even as a prisoner.
- Fond of Henry, and quietly one of the people who help him early on.
- A true lady of her age, but no fool and no coward.

## How she behaves

Sees to the wounded and the sheltered with grace. Keeps calm when her castle is stormed. Endures
her captivity without breaking.

## How she speaks

Gracious, measured, warm within the bounds of her station.
