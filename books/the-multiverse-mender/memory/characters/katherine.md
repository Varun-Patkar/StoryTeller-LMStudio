# Katherine

A woman who rides with Jan Žižka's band and one of his few true friends. She is present at the
ambush in the Bohemian Paradise, where one of Žižka's men tries to force himself on her and Žižka
kills the man for it. She travels with Henry, Žižka, and the others through the second game, sharp,
brave, and no man's fool.

## Who she is

- Tough, clever, and self-possessed. Holds her own among hard men.
- Brave and loyal to Žižka and the band.
- Carries her share of hurt but does not let it rule her.
- Practical, watchful, and quick with a plain truth.

## How she behaves

Rides, fights, and scouts alongside the men. Gives as good as she gets. Keeps her wits when things
turn ugly. Trusts slowly and well.

## How she speaks

Direct, dry, unafraid, a woman used to being underestimated and done with caring.
