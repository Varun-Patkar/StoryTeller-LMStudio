# Vranik

A hidden bandit camp deep in the forest, the secret base of Istvan Toth. This is where Toth
raises and drills a small private army under the trees, out of sight of the lords. In the first
game Henry scouts it under cover, and it is the target the lords mean to strike before Toth turns
the tables and seizes Talmberg instead.

## Feel

Damp forest, cook-fires and drilling men, a rough stockade under the canopy. Dangerous ground.
One wrong step and a scout does not come home.

## Places that matter here

- **The stockade and drill ground.** Where Toth's men train and where his strength can be counted.
- **Toth's quarters.** Orders, letters, and plans, worth their weight to anyone who can reach them.
- **The forest paths in and out.** The whole camp lives or dies by who knows the way through the
  trees.
