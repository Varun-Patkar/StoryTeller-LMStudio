# Villages and lesser places of the first game

The smaller settlements around Rattay and Sasau where much of the first game's work is done.
Grouped here to keep them handy.

## Uzhitz

A farming village with a church and a tavern. Home to the drinking, worldly priest Father Godwin.
Henry helps with a string of village troubles here, from a witch-hunt to Godwin's own bad habits.
Ordinary people, ordinary sins, honest ground.

## Merhojed

A small village struck by pestilence in the first game. Henry has to find the source of the
sickness and a cure while the place dies around him. A grim, frightened spot when the plague hits.

## Neuhof

A stud farm where the region's warhorses are raised. Raided by Cumans, its horses stolen, in one
of the first game's threads. Open pasture, stables, and the smell of horse. A good horse is worth
a fortune to a fighting man.

## Pribyslavitz

A ruined, abandoned village that can be rebuilt into a working settlement over the course of the
first game's Pribyslavitz thread. A blank slate, a place to make something new out of nothing.

## Ledetchko and the mills

A small village with a mill by the water near Rattay. Millers here, as everywhere, keep one foot
in honest trade and one in the shadier sort, which makes them useful to a man who needs a quiet
hand.
