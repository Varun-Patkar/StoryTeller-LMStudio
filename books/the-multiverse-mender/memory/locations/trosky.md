# Trosky and the Bohemian Paradise

The country of the second game, east of the first game's valley. Rocky, wooded, and beautiful,
with strange stone towers rising out of the trees. The people call it the Bohemian Paradise.

## The Bohemian Paradise

Wild rock country of pillars, gorges, and thick forest. It looks like a garden and hides plenty
of teeth. This is where Henry and Hans are ambushed on the road to Trosky, their escort cut down
and their gear stripped, by the outlaws who turn out to be Jan Žižka's loyalist band. Good ground
for an ambush, and good ground to disappear in.

## Trosky Castle

A great castle built on two rock spires, with two towers called Baba and Panna. Seat of Otto von
Bergow, Lord of Trosky and a high man in Sigismund's council. In the second game it is both a
prison and a lion's den. Henry is forced to serve von Bergow, is thrown into its dungeons, and
escapes. In the tower, Henry and Istvan Toth have their last reckoning, and Toth is thrown from a
high window to his death.

## Nebakov

A place with a mill, where von Bergow sends Henry and Hans against so-called outlaws who prove to
be the armed hand of the loyalists. The Battle of Nebakov Mill is fought here, and afterward Henry
and Žižka duel, with Henry wounding Žižka's eye.

## Feel

Green and grey, stone and forest, mist in the gorges. Beautiful and dangerous in the same breath.
Great lords scheming behind thick walls while common men bleed in the woods below.
