# Sasau Monastery

A large Benedictine monastery on the river, still half-built, with a bustling town growing at
its feet. In the first game Henry goes undercover here as a novice monk to hunt a hidden enemy,
living the strict round of prayer, work, and silence while he searches. The place is a maze of
cloisters, cells, a library, and a crypt.

The town below has a fine blacksmith, a codex maker, an executioner, and a busy market. It is
one of the richest spots in the region.

## Feel

Chanting and bells at every hour, cold cloisters, candle wax and old books. Below the walls, a
loud market and the ring of the best forge around. Piety on top, ordinary human sin underneath.

## Places that matter here

- **The cloister and cells.** Where the monks live the day by the bell, and where a spy must
  learn the rules fast or be caught.
- **The library and scriptorium.** Books, learning, and secrets kept in ink.
- **The town forge and market.** Top-quality steel and trade for anyone with coin.
