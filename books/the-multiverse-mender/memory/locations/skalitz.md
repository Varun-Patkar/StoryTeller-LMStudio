# Silver Skalitz

A small silver-mining town in the valley, held by Sir Radzig Kobyla. This is home. The pits
bring up good silver, so the town is richer than its size, which is exactly why an army wants
it. There is a church, an inn, a tanner, a mill on the stream, and Martin's smithy, the best
forge for miles. The people are miners, tradesmen, and farmers who mostly keep to their own.

For Hugo it is the whole world at first, and then it is ash. The raid burns it flat. After the
war, Skalitz sits half-ruined and haunted, a place people pass through and do not stay.

## Feel

Woodsmoke, hot iron, the ring of the hammer, dust from the mine road. Bells for Mass. Loud on
market day, quiet and dark after dark. Once the raid comes, it is screaming, then silence, then
crows.

## Places that matter here

- **Martin's smithy.** The family forge. Hugo grows up with soot under his nails and learns the
  hammer honestly, which the system rewards.
- **The church and its bell.** Where the town gathers, and where the dying flee when the Cumans
  come.
- **The mine and the miners' road.** The reason the town is rich and the reason it burns.
- **The mill and the stream below town.** Quiet ground, good for slipping in and out unseen.

## What Hugo knows is here

Hugo carries the memory of this land from the game he once played, so he knows where useful
things lie waiting before anyone else thinks to look. None of it is magic. It is all things a
sharp, well-informed man could find:

- Buried caches and hidden chests in the woods and old camps around the valley, the kind marked
  on treasure maps. Some are locked and need a good hand with lockpicks. Some are buried and
  need a spade.
- Cuman and bandit camps in the forest, worth good coin and good steel to a man willing to take
  them, or to rob while their owners sleep.
- Wild herbs for brewing along the streams and clearings, the makings of healing draughts and
  poisons for a patient hand.
- The tools and stock of the smithy itself, and the quiet knowledge of which travelling trader
  carries what.

Hugo uses this the honest, careful way. He does not get rich overnight. He gets ready.
