# Kuttenberg

The great silver city, the richest place in the story and the heart of the second game's second
half. A real city, not a town: stone houses, a cathedral rising, a mint that stamps the kingdom's
coin, mines under the ground, a Jewish quarter, taverns, and merchant palaces. Where there is this
much silver there is this much greed, and the whole city runs on it.

Henry follows a knot of threads here: a spy for Lord John of Liechtenstein, a fraud in the mines
and the mint, the merchant family of the Ruthards, and the hidden man Samuel who turns out to be
his own half-brother. Later Sigismund moves to rob the city's Jews, and Henry races to save them.

## Feel

Crowds, coin, and church bells. The reek of the smelters and the mine. Fine clothes and cut
throats on the same street. A place where a poor man can rise or vanish, both quickly.

## Places that matter here

- **The mint and the mines.** The source of all the silver and all the trouble. Fraud, murder,
  and politics run through them.
- **The Ruthard palace.** Home of the merchant Kunzlin Ruthard and his daughter Rosa.
- **The Jewish quarter and the synagogue.** Where Samuel and the hidden threads live, and where
  Sigismund's cruelty falls hardest.
- **The taverns.** The Hangman's Noose, The Hole, King Solomon's, the Black Mare. Where news and
  spies and rough men gather.
