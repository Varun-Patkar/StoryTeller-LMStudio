# Other places of the second game

The lesser sites around Trosky and Kuttenberg, grouped here.

## Suchdol

A village and its ground where the war comes to its head. Henry travels here with Žižka and
Katherine, meets Peter of Písek, and speaks again with Jobst of Moravia. The great Battle of
Suchdol, where the loyalist cause makes its stand against Sigismund's forces and Otto von Bergow,
is fought here and decides the fate of the region.

## Devil's Den

A hidden camp and gathering place where Henry and Hans and their allies rest, plan, and gather
strength in the second game. A rough, safe hole away from the great lords' eyes.

## Maleszov

A holding where Hans Capon is kept prisoner in the second game, and from which Henry frees him.

## Semine and the villages

Farming country and small settlements around the region, the ordinary Bohemian world of fields,
mills, taverns, and hard-working folk that the war rolls over.
