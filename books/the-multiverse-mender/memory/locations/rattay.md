# Rattay

A proper town, the seat of Sir Hanush of Leipa, and the loyalist heart of the region after
Skalitz falls. Sir Radzig gathers what forces remain here. This is where Henry becomes a fighting
man under Captain Bernard, and where much of the first game's business is done: the garrison, the
tavern, the bathhouse, the market, the mill.

Two towns really, an upper and a lower, joined by a bridge, sitting where two rivers meet. Busy,
ordered, and watched. For Hugo it is the place where a smith's son can become something more, if
he is patient and useful.

## Feel

River damp, market noise, guards on the walls. Ale and sweat in the tavern. The clack of the
training yard. A working town at the edge of a war.

## Places that matter here

- **The garrison and training yard.** Captain Bernard's ground. Where honest sweat turns a boy
  into a soldier, which the system likes.
- **Sir Hanush's court.** Where the lords argue and plan the war.
- **The tavern and bathhouse.** Where news, drink, and other comforts change hands.
- **The mill.** Millers keep to the shadier trades, useful to a man who needs things done quietly.
