# Talmberg

A stone castle on a hill, held by Sir Divish and his wife Lady Stephanie. It looks down over the
valley and its walls are strong. When Skalitz burns, Talmberg is the nearest safe place, and its
gate is where Henry drags himself half-dead in the game. In this story it is where Hugo brings
the whole family when they flee.

Later in the first game's ending, Istvan Toth storms and takes Talmberg, holding Lady Stephanie
and Sir Radzig hostage, and the lords have to besiege their own ally's castle to win them back.

## Feel

Cold stone, torch smoke, the smell of horses and men-at-arms. A place of walls and watch-fires.
Safe, but not warm. Refugees crowd the bailey after the raid, frightened and hungry.

## Places that matter here

- **The gate and the drawbridge.** The line between living and dying on the day Skalitz falls.
- **The armoury.** Good gear for a man who can talk or sneak his way to it.
- **Sir Divish's hall.** Where the lord and lady hold court and where hard news is carried.
