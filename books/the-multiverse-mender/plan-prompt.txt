Build a full chapter-by-chapter plan for Arc One of this story: the entire Kingdom Come saga,
both games, from the fall of Skalitz to the end of the war. Read config.md, canon.md, and
everything in memory/ first. Keep it an outline, not prose. Follow the canon in canon.md as
true EXCEPT where this plan changes it below. Do not write the separate prologue (the gamer's
death and meeting the god); this arc begins in Skalitz.

Point of view stays close on Hugo, third person. The system appears as short bracketed, bolded
notes only when it matters, never crowding the scene. No em dashes anywhere. Simple, grounded
words in the register of the game.

The changes this plan must weave through the canon:

- Hugo is the true-born son of Martin and Jane, and Henry's younger half-brother by one year
  (they share their mother; Henry's blood father is Radzig, unknown to all). Hugo's memories of
  his past life and of this world wake at twelve after a fall that nearly kills him, giving him
  six years before Skalitz burns. Henry is nineteen and Hugo eighteen when it falls.

- In those six years Hugo cannot warn anyone; he is only a smith's son and no lord would believe
  him. So he prepares quietly. He trains in secret, grows real skill at the forge, the blade,
  the bow, and uses his memory of this world to find hidden caches, camps, herbs, and coin near
  Skalitz to arm and ready the family. Show the strain of knowing what is coming and being unable
  to say it.

- When Skalitz falls, Hugo saves Martin, Jane, and Henry, and gets them all out to Talmberg while
  fleeing. The parents live.

- Henry's parents live, so his revenge is real but less consuming than in canon. His drive is
  vengeance for Bianca, his sweetheart killed in the raid, and for the people of Skalitz who
  died. His canon target stays the same: Sir Markvart von Aulitz, the general who led the sack.

- Henry must still walk his own canon road. He goes back to the ruins to search for survivors,
  and his father's sword is stolen there by the bandits Runt and Zbyshek. Hugo shadows him and
  helps save him alongside Theresa, the two of them holding on until Captain Bernard arrives.
  Hugo allows Henry's story to happen; he does not live it for him.

- Istvan Toth dies before the end of the first game, at the Vranik and Talmberg climax, rather
  than surviving to Trosky. Remove Toth from all second-game events.

- Because Toth is gone, the second game shifts: the lords' message actually reaches Trosky and is
  delivered to Otto von Bergow, but Henry and Hans are still ambushed by Zizka's band in the
  Bohemian Paradise. There Hugo makes clear to Zizka which side they are on, that they stand for
  Wenceslaus and that Otto and Sigismund are the enemy. No Toth meddles now. Erik is at the
  ambush but recognises Hans and his band and slips away rather than fight. Erik dies later,
  during the second game.

- Henry learns near the end of the first game that Radzig is his true father, and with it that he
  and Hugo are half-brothers through their mother. But the truth of what Hugo really is, a
  traveller across worlds, is revealed only at the very end of the whole arc, and only to Henry.

- Hugo has a brief, honest fling with Rosa Ruthard in Kuttenberg (the romance canon gives Henry).
  Both know it goes nowhere: for her a matter of station, for him because he will not be here
  forever, though he cannot tell her why.

- The whole of both games must be experienced across the plan. End the arc where the war ends:
  Erik dead, Henry going home to the mill with Theresa and their living parents to a normal life.
  Hugo tells Henry the truth, says his goodbye, and leaves this world. That final reveal and
  farewell is the last beat of the arc.

Structure the plan so the six preparation years, the fall of Skalitz, the whole first game, the
whole second game, and the ending each get their due weight, as a numbered chapter-by-chapter
outline.
