# Mara Ellery

<!-- Human-written memory. Facts that must stay true across chapters, not a biography essay. -->

- Returned to the town after eleven years away; won't say why.
- Runs the shuttered bookshop her aunt left her, mostly as an excuse to be left alone.
- Reads people fast, trusts them slowly.
- Speaks in short sentences. Deflects with dry humour when cornered.
- Keeps a tide table in her coat pocket out of habit, not need.
